1. Begin with the problem this topic solves.
2. Learn the vocabulary in the context of a small example.

## Practical activity

Design researcher, writer, and reviewer roles with explicit inputs, outputs, and approval gates.

Before you finish, write down the input, expected output, assumptions, and at least two checks that would reveal an incorrect result.

## Common mistakes

- Giving tools broader permissions than necessary.
- Running loops without limits or stop conditions.
- Automating consequential actions without review.
